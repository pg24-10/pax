FAQs

Good questions.

Does Pax cost anything?

No. Planning, voting and booking with Pax is free. You pay the same price you would pay booking on MakeMyTrip yourself.

Do my friends need the MakeMyTrip app?

No. They tap the group link you forward, then reply to Pax in their own WhatsApp chat. No app, no login.

How many people can come?

Up to 8 people per trip, including you.

Is my money safe?

Yes. When you pledge, your share is only blocked in your own bank account with a UPI One-Time Mandate that you approve with your PIN. It is charged only when the trip is booked, and never more than you approved. If you don't go, the block is released.

Can my friends see how I voted?

No. You vote in your private chat with Pax. The group only sees a count, like "4 of 5 voted".

What if we can't agree?

Pick "none of these" and Pax brings 3 new options. If the group goes quiet, the best-fit option goes ahead for everyone who pledged.

What happens after the trip is booked?

Pax stays in the group until everyone is home: reminders, who arrives when, a shared kitty for cabs and food, and settling up at the end with a UPI request for exactly what each person owes.
