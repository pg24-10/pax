faq

ok but...

Is my money safe?

Yes. When you pledge, your share is only blocked in your own bank account using a UPI One-Time Mandate that you approve with your PIN. Nothing is debited until the trip is booked. If you don't go, Pax releases it. Payments run on Pine Labs.

Can my friends see how I voted?

No. Votes happen in your 1:1 chat with Pax. The group only sees a counter, like "4 of 5 voted".

Do I need to download anything?

No. Pax lives on WhatsApp. If you can send a message, you can plan a trip.

What if we just can't decide?

Pick "none of the above" and Pax brings 3 new options. If the group still can't decide, the best-fit option goes ahead for everyone who pledged. Nobody is dragged along without saying yes.

What if the group picks a trip I didn't vote for?

You get 48 hours to join or pass. No awkwardness, no pressure.
